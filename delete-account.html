Delete your NearTask account

NearTask is run by Artwin Ltd (company no. 13371113). You can delete your account and data at any time.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open NearTask and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account, then confirm.

By email

If you can't sign in, email support@artwin.co.uk from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We reply within one month.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos and work photos
  • Your listings, services, offers and quotes
  • Your messages and notifications
  • Your saved searches, favourites and phone notification settings

Problem reports are kept only as long as needed to deal with them, and no longer than 2 years. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.
